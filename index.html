Michael Wood

Renewable energy engineer. Lecco, Italy.

I design and optimize distributed energy systems: microgrids, load and PV forecasting, and the dispatch decisions in between.

About

It started around age ten, watching my grandfather swap the battery in our little fishing boat. He touched the wrench across both terminals and showered us in sparks. I was more intrigued than scared.

Since then I've taken apart computers, built a solar cell from scratch, and installed hydroelectric turbines in Rwanda. Now I do it with fancier tools, and I'm forever grateful for that original spark.

Away from the screen: ski touring and mountaineering, bike touring, climbing, ukulele, gluten-free cooking, photography, and an unreasonable number of audiobooks.
